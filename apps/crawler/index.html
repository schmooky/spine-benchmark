Pixi Crawler

Real-time scene-graph profiler for PixiJS + Spine

Crawler continuously scans your PixiJS stage for performance issues - broken batches, hidden-but-updating skeletons, deep nesting, off-screen objects, excessive children, and more. Every issue is categorised, highlighted directly on the canvas, and scored with a Rendering Impact metric so you know what to fix first.

This demo builds a 12-cell showcase where each cell intentionally triggers a specific issue category. Open the overlay and walk through them to see how Crawler surfaces problems in a real scene.
